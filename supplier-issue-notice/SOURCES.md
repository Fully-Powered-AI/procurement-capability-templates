From the supplier-correspondence method in panaversity/agentfactory-business-plugins (Apache-2.0) it takes the two kinds of notice and their shape: an invoice dispute that sets each discrepancy against the purchase order or the goods received with its amount and its basis, says what the supplier should do and by when, and keeps the undisputed amount apart; and a corrective-action request that sets a measured result for a stated period against the agreed target, with the records behind it, and asks for a written plan covering cause, protection of current orders, fixes with dates and ongoing monitoring, with an optional review call. It also takes the factual tone without blame, sign-off by an approver chosen by kind of notice, and the rule that a change of bank details is confirmed by phone on a known number, never by reply. Fully Powered AI added the working of every figure from the documents (the billable quantity as the least of invoiced, ordered and received, a quantity part and a price part on each line, earlier invoices counted), the price tolerance with its list of items not raised, the tie-out of disputed and undisputed amounts against the accepted lines, the reply date counted in the buyer's working days, the cover sheet for the approver with its checks, stops and decisions, the recalculation of on-time and rejection rates from delivery records with a stop when the target is met, column headers that must match or be mapped, evidence limited to documents supplied, and the refusal of breach claims, penalties and notices that end or change a supply relationship. The source's non-renewal, supply-assurance and exit notices are left out. No third-party text is reused.
