From the vendor review method in anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (Apache-2.0) it takes looking at a renewal through cost, performance and risk together, counting charges beyond the subscription and the costs of leaving alongside the price, weighing the renewal price against what is paid today, and ending with points to raise with the supplier. From the vendor management method in alirezarezvani/claude-skills (MIT) it takes checking a supplier against the service commitments it made, telling recent misses from older ones, listing service credits a person still has to claim, grading how far the business depends on the supplier and whether a written fallback exists, and leaving the decision with a person. Fully Powered AI narrowed the work to one supplier on one page, and added the notice and decide-by dates counted only from dates the person supplies, the count of what is paid for against what is used, the invoice and credit checks, questions that each rest on a fact, and the rules that no agreement is read or interpreted and that nothing is sent, accepted, cancelled or claimed. No third-party text is reused.
