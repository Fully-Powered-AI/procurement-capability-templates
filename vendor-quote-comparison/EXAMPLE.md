# Vendor quote comparison: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The firm, the vendors, the quotes, the notes and every figure below are invented. The firm is an invented 28-person accounting practice choosing a new office-cleaning contractor. Its office manager gathered six quotes; the managing partner chooses, and the firm's outside lawyer reads contract terms. People are referred to by role and vendors by letter.

The inputs come first, in full, so the template can be run on them. The comparison sheet follows.

## The inputs

### 1. Purchase brief

```
What: evening office cleaning, one floor, 6,000 sq ft, five nights a week.
Reason: the current contractor's agreement ends on 30 November 2026.
Start wanted: on or before Tuesday 1 December 2026.
Decision needed by: Friday 20 November 2026.
Commitment wanted: 12 months.
Prepared by: office manager, Monday 9 November 2026.
Currency: US dollars.
```

### 2. Criteria sheet (agreed with the managing partner on Monday 2 November 2026, before any quote was compared)

```
Must-haves
M1  Cleaning starts no earlier than 6:00 pm on weekdays.
M2  The quote states general liability cover of at least $1,000,000.
M3  Service can start on or before 1 December 2026.

Comparison criteria
Cost over the term: lower is better.
C1  Restroom service, days per week: higher is better.
C2  Time to fix a reported missed clean, hours: lower is better.

Budget ceiling: $28,000 over 12 months.
Cost period: 12 months from the start date.
Every price must cover: restroom and kitchen supplies (paper, soap, bin
  liners), either included or priced separately.
```

### 3. The quotes (typed copies; the order is the order received)

```

serviced five days a week. Missed cleans fixed within 24 hours of a

$2,000,000. Earliest start 16 November 2026. Carpet shampoo once a year
included. This agreement renews automatically for further 12-month
periods unless either party gives 60 days' written notice before the
renewal date. Rates may increase by up to 5% at each renewal.

### 4. Notes from calls (typed by the office manager)

```

  restrooms six days a week at no extra cost if we asked.

## The comparison sheet

**VENDOR QUOTE COMPARISON: evening office cleaning, one floor, 6,000 sq ft, five nights a week**

- **For:** managing partner. Prepared by: office manager on 9 November 2026. Decision needed by: 20 November 2026.
- **Criteria agreed:** 2 November 2026, by the managing partner. Criteria changed after the quotes were read: none.
- **Quotes compared:** 6. Notes: supplied.
- Vendor A: quote dated 4 November 2026, valid until 18 November 2026 (14 days from the quote date), start 16 November 2026. **EXPIRES BEFORE THE DECISION DATE**
- Vendor B: quote dated 3 November 2026, valid until 30 November 2026, start 23 November 2026.
- Vendor C: quote dated 5 November 2026, valid until 15 December 2026, start 1 December 2026.
- Vendor D: quote dated 2 November 2026, valid until 2 December 2026, start 23 November 2026.
- Vendor E: quote dated 6 November 2026, valid until 6 December 2026, start 1 December 2026.
- Vendor F: quote dated 4 November 2026, valid until 4 December 2026, start 30 November 2026.

**The criteria**

- Must-haves: M1 cleaning starts no earlier than 6:00 pm on weekdays. M2 the quote states general liability cover of at least \$1,000,000. M3 service can start on or before 1 December 2026.
- Comparison: cost over 12 months, lower is better. C1 restroom service, days per week, higher is better. C2 time to fix a reported missed clean, hours, lower is better.
- Budget ceiling: \$28,000 over 12 months. Every price must cover: restroom and kitchen supplies.

**Cost over the term**

- Vendor A: \$600 one-off + \$2,150 × 12 = \$25,800; supplies included = **\$26,400**.
- Vendor B: \$1,780 × 12 = \$21,360; supplies included = **\$21,360**.
- Vendor C: \$1,950 × 6 = \$11,700 + \$2,028 (after the 4% rise) × 6 = \$12,168 + supplies \$140 × 12 = \$1,680 = **\$25,548**.
- Vendor D: \$2,300 × 12 = \$27,600; supplies included = **\$27,600**.
- Vendor E: \$400 one-off + \$1,690 × 12 = \$20,280; supplies included = **\$20,680**.
- Vendor F: \$2,600 × 12 = \$31,200; supplies included = **\$31,200**.

**Side by side**

- Vendor A: M1 "crew arrives 6:30 pm". M2 "\$2,000,000". M3 "16 November 2026". Cost \$26,400. C1 5. C2 24. Also offered, not compared: carpet shampoo once a year.
- Vendor B: M1 "5:00 pm to 9:00 pm". M2 "\$1,000,000". M3 "23 November 2026". Cost \$21,360. C1 5. C2 12. Also offered, not compared: none.
- Vendor C: M1 "from 7:00 pm". M2 "\$1,000,000". M3 "1 December 2026". Cost \$25,548. C1 3. C2 12. Also offered, not compared: none.
- Vendor D: M1 "no earlier than 6:00 pm". M2 "\$1,000,000". M3 "23 November 2026". Cost \$27,600. C1 5. C2 24. Also offered, not compared: none.
- Vendor E: M1 "crew arrives 6:30 pm". M2 not stated. M3 "1 December 2026". Cost \$20,680. C1 5. C2 24. Also offered, not compared: none. Commitment offered: "Service is month to month"; wanted: 12 months.
- Vendor F: M1 "from 8:00 pm". M2 "\$2,000,000". M3 "30 November 2026". Cost \$31,200. C1 5. C2 4. Also offered, not compared: interior window cleaning every quarter.

**Not counted**

- Vendor A: restrooms six days a week at no extra cost. Not in writing: in the notes of 5 November, not in the quote. Bears on C1.
- Vendor C: "Rated the region's top cleaner by our customers." Vendor's own claim, not checked. Bears on no criterion.
- Vendor E: "we're fully insured, no problem". Not in writing: in the notes of 6 November, not in the quote. Bears on M2.

**Round 1: must-haves**

- Eliminated: Vendor B, M1: "Our crews work 5:00 pm to 9:00 pm".
- On hold: Vendor E, M2: in the notes only; the quote does not state the cover.
- Passed: A, C, D, F.

**Round 2: budget**

- Eliminated: Vendor F, \$31,200, \$3,200 over the ceiling.
- On hold: none.
- Passed: A, C, D.

**Round 3: outperformed**

- Compared: A, C, D.
- Eliminated: Vendor D, outperformed by Vendor A: cost \$26,400 against \$27,600; C1 5 against 5; C2 24 against 24.
- Not comparable: none.

**Where each vendor stands**

- Vendor A: shortlisted.
- Vendor B: eliminated in round 1: crews work from 5:00 pm; M1 needs no earlier than 6:00 pm.
- Vendor C: shortlisted.
- Vendor D: eliminated in round 3: outperformed by Vendor A, which costs \$1,200 less and matches it on C1 and C2.
- Vendor E: on hold: the quote does not state general liability cover (M2). Cost over the term \$20,680.
- Vendor F: eliminated in round 2: \$31,200, \$3,200 over the \$28,000 ceiling.

**The shortlist**

- Vendor A: better on C1, restrooms 5 days a week against 3 for Vendor C. Worse on cost, \$26,400 against \$25,548 (\$852 more), and on C2, 24 hours against 12.
- Vendor C: better on cost, \$25,548 against \$26,400 (\$852 less), and on C2, 12 hours against 24. Worse on C1, restrooms 3 days a week against 5.

**For the contract reviewer**

- Vendor A: "This agreement renews automatically for further 12-month periods unless either party gives 60 days' written notice before the renewal date." "Rates may increase by up to 5% at each renewal."
- Vendor C: "Rates increase by 4% from the seventh month of service." "Either party may end this agreement with 30 days' written notice."
- Vendor E: "Service is month to month."
- Quoted for the firm's outside lawyer; not interpreted.

**Questions for vendors (drafts for a person to send)**

- Vendor E, M2: Please confirm in writing, as part of your quote, the general liability cover you carry and its amount. We need at least \$1,000,000 stated in the quote.
- Vendor A, C1: On our call of 5 November you mentioned restroom service six days a week at no extra cost. If that is your offer, please add it to the written quote.
- Vendor A, quote validity: Your quote is valid until 18 November and our decision is due on 20 November. Can you extend it to 20 November?

**Decisions for the managing partner**

1. Choose between Vendor A (\$26,400, restrooms 5 days a week, missed cleans fixed within 24 hours) and Vendor C (\$25,548, restrooms 3 days a week, fixed within 12 hours), or wait for Vendor E's answer on insurance before choosing. Vendor E's cost over the term is \$20,680.
2. Vendor A's quote expires on 18 November, two days before the decision date: decide by 18 November, or wait for the answer on an extension.

Before anything is signed, the firm's outside lawyer reads the terms above.

Status: prepared for the managing partner's choice. Nothing has been sent, accepted, ordered or signed.
