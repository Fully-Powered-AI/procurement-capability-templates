# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. Two published methods shaped it. From the vendor-management guide in finsilabs/awesome-ecommerce-skills (MIT) it takes reviewing suppliers every quarter; working out punctuality, completeness and damage from the business's own order and receiving records, each against a target; following an order that arrives in several deliveries line by line; treating the supplier's confirmation of each order as something worth tracking; the point that an order's due date and the day the stock was truly wanted can differ, so punctuality says nothing about the second; and taking the figures into the conversation with the supplier. From the vendor-assessment method in panaversity/agentfactory-business-plugins (Apache-2.0) it takes keeping the question of whether the business could manage without a supplier apart from the size of the bill, so an irreplaceable supplier with a small bill still gets full attention; raising a figure that is slipping quarter on quarter before it crosses its target; and the rule that leaving a supplier is not on the table until someone has a replacement ready. Fully Powered AI wrote the rest: the self-contained form that works from stated export headers and stops when one does not match; price measured against the business's own agreed-price list with a tolerance, and the amount above it added up line by line; a minimum number of lines before a measure is judged; every miss listed by order line as the evidence for its figure; the comparison with last quarter's scorecard with a band for level; a count of targets met in place of one blended score; points to raise written from the evidence; decision questions for a warning, a change of tier, a move away or a missing alternative, each raised only when its trigger is met and each with its facts; and the rule that nothing is sent to a supplier and no tier or record is changed. No third-party text is reused.
