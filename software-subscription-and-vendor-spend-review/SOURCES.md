From the go-to-market spend management method in LeadMagic/gtm-skills (MIT), at skills/gtm-ops/gtm-spend-management, it takes keeping one list of every paid tool with its owner, renewal date and seats paid against seats in use, and reviewing that list on a regular cycle for tools nobody owns, tools whose owner has left the company, tools that overlap and renewals coming up, along with treating tools paid on a personal card or an expense claim as hidden spend to bring to light. From the procurement optimizer in alirezarezvani/claude-skills (MIT), at business-operations/skills/procurement-optimizer, it takes classing spend by the kind of thing bought, whatever the supplier is called, treating tools that do the same job as a consolidation question that has to count the cost of switching, holding back any consolidation of a critical tool until a written fallback exists, and watching for months where several renewals fall together. Fully Powered AI widened the work from sales software to every subscription, and added reading a plain card or ledger export with stated column headers, the rules that tell monthly, annual, irregular and one-off charges apart, the annual cost with its stated basis, the flags for a double charge and a charge that stopped, the saving range that never names a tool to keep, questions that each rest on a fact, and the rule that nothing is cancelled, changed or paid. No third-party text is reused.
