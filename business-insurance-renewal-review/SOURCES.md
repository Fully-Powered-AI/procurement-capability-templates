From the insurance programme method in CaseMark/skills (Apache-2.0) it takes gathering every policy into a single list before the renewal, weighing the business's revenue, payroll, vehicles and sites against what is insured, noting the amounts of cover that customer contracts call for, totalling claims by type of cover and squaring them with the business's own records, and checking that each figure comes from the current documents. From the startup insurance guide in LeadMagic/gtm-skills (MIT) it takes the points that customers often want a named amount of cover and to be named on the policy, that what the insurer is told about the business should be kept up to date, that a policy's exclusions need reading alongside its cost, and that cover deserves a fresh look as the business grows. Fully Powered AI wrote the pack's form for the owner who meets the broker, the item-by-item marking of every change between this year's schedules and the renewal terms, the dates counted from the renewal date, the questions that each rest on a fact, and the rules that no policy wording is interpreted, no cover is recommended and nothing is sent, accepted, paid or reported.
