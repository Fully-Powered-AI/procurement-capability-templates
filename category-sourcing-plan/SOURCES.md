From the strategic-sourcing method in kishorkukreja/awesome-supply-chain (MIT) it takes starting a category's plan from its spend (including its share of company spend and its top three suppliers' combined share), its suppliers and the people who rely on it; placing the category on the widely used matrix of supply risk against spend impact, with a usual direction for each of the four positions; sorting value levers into groups for price, demand and specification, the supplier base, total cost and working with suppliers; agreeing the starting prices before any sourcing and not counting a fall in volume as a price saving; expecting users to push back on a change of supplier or specification; and the usual order of sourcing steps, from finding new suppliers through quotes and negotiation and a decision with contract review to moving orders and checking results. Fully Powered AI designed the spend checks against the export and its default column headers, the five supply-risk factors each rated against a stated limit, the separate handling of items only one supplier can make, the rule that only the gap to the lowest price the company itself already paid is given a figure, the options that always include keeping the current arrangement, the dates given to each of the six steps, with every supplier date flagged against them, and the rules that a person chooses the approach, agreements go to a reviewer uninterpreted, and nothing is sent, ordered or changed.
