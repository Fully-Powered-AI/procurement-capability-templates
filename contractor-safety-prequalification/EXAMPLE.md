# Contractor safety prequalification: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The company, the contractor, the people, the documents, the dates and every figure below are invented. The company is an invented food distribution business with two warehouses. Its facilities manager is bringing in an electrical contractor it has not used before to replace failed light fittings high above a working warehouse floor. Its outside health and safety adviser decides. Organisations are referred to by letter.

The example has two runs. First come the inputs for the first run, in full, and the questionnaire it returned. Then come the contractor's answers and documents, in full, and the evaluation record the second run returned.

## Inputs for the first run

### 1. Job brief (typed by the facilities manager)

```
Contractor: Contractor E, an electrical contractor we have not used before.
Job: replace 14 failed LED high-bay light fittings in Warehouse 2 and
  re-wire two lighting circuits.
Activities:
  - working from a mobile elevating work platform (MEWP) at about 9 m to
    remove and fit the light fittings;
  - re-wiring the two lighting circuits, which our maintenance team will
    isolate and lock off before work starts.
Site and area: Warehouse 2, aisles 8 to 14.
Dates: Monday 7 December 2026 to Friday 11 December 2026.
Who else could be affected: Warehouse 2 stays open; pickers and forklifts
  work in the aisles next to the platform.
Decision type: new approval, for this job and similar lighting work next
  year.
Questionnaire back by: Wednesday 18 November 2026, to the facilities manager.
Decides: health and safety adviser.
Chases conditions: facilities manager.
Prepared by: facilities manager, Monday 9 November 2026.
```

### 2. Prequalification settings (the company's standing settings)

```
High-hazard list: hot work; confined spaces; work at height; lifting with
  cranes or hoists; work on or near live electrical systems; demolition;
  excavation; asbestos or other substances that need special control.
Insurance: general liability at least $2,000,000 each occurrence;
  workers' compensation required, no amount set.
Safety record look-back: 3 years.
Must-pass questions: C1, C2, C4, and H1 and H2 for each high-hazard activity.
Stop findings: (a) the contractor states it does not hold a required cover;
  (b) a regulator notice, citation or prosecution in the look-back period
  that the contractor's records show as still open; (c) for a Tier 3
  activity, nobody named for it holds any certification for it.
Conditions close: before work starts, unless the health and safety adviser
  sets a later date.
Review cadence: 12 months.
Currency: US dollars.
```

## What the first run returned: the questionnaire

**CONTRACTOR SAFETY PREQUALIFICATION: Contractor E for replacement of 14 high-bay light fittings and re-wiring of two lighting circuits, Warehouse 2**

- Stage: Questionnaire to issue. Prepared by: facilities manager on 9 November 2026. Decision by: health and safety adviser.
- Job: replace 14 failed LED high-bay light fittings and re-wire two lighting circuits. Site and area: Warehouse 2, aisles 8 to 14. Dates: 7 December 2026 to 11 December 2026. Decision type: new approval, for this job and similar lighting work next year.
- Who else could be affected: pickers and forklifts working in the aisles next to the platform; Warehouse 2 stays open.
- Settings: as supplied.

**RISK TIER**

- Working from a mobile elevating work platform at about 9 m: Tier 3, on the high-hazard list as work at height.
- Re-wiring two lighting circuits, isolated and locked off before work starts: Tier 2, skilled trade, tools or plant, nothing on the high-hazard list.
- Tier for the job: Tier 3, set by working from the platform. Questions asked: C1 to C4, T1 to T4, H1 to H3 (work at height).

**PART A: QUESTIONNAIRE FOR THE CONTRACTOR**

To: Contractor E. Return by: 18 November 2026. Return to: facilities manager.

How to answer:

- Put the question code on every document you attach.
- We score only what is attached: an answer with nothing behind it is treated as unanswered.
- Each certificate and policy has to stay valid from 7 December 2026 to 11 December 2026, not just today.
- Answer every question. If one does not fit this job, write one line beside its code saying why. A plain "no" counts once you sign below.
- Leave out injury and health details about anyone; call an injured person Worker 1, Worker 2.

- **C1** Which insurance do you hold for this work, with what limits, and until when? Evidence that answers it: a certificate for each required cover showing the insurer, the limit and the expiry date. **MUST PASS**
- **C2** Over the last 3 years, which injuries to your workers or to others had to be reported to a safety regulator, and which notices, citations or prosecutions did you receive? Which are still open? Evidence that answers it: your incident summary for the period, signed by an owner or director, and a copy of each notice or citation with its closure letter if it is closed. **MUST PASS**
- **C3** Who in your company is responsible for safety, and what is your written policy? Evidence that answers it: the policy, signed and dated, naming the person or the role responsible.
- **C4** What trade licences, registrations or training for their trade do the people doing this job hold? Evidence that answers it: the people-on-this-job list and a copy of each trade licence, registration or training record on it. **MUST PASS**
- **T1** How will you do this job, step by step, and what are its risks and their controls? Evidence that answers it: a method statement and risk assessment written for this job at this site.
- **T2** Who will supervise your people on site, and how often will they be there? Evidence that answers it: the supervisor's row on the people-on-this-job list and their qualification record.
- **T3** Will any of the work be subcontracted? If so, to whom, and how do you check their safety? Evidence that answers it: each subcontractor's name and your check of it, or a signed "no".
- **T4** What powered tools, plant or access equipment will you bring, and when was each last inspected? Evidence that answers it: the latest inspection or examination record for each item, with its next due date.
- **H1 (work at height)** Which of your people will carry out work at height, and what certification for it does each hold? Evidence that answers it: a current card or certificate for work at height for each of them, in date for the whole job. **MUST PASS**
- **H2 (work at height)** What will you put in place to remove or reduce the hazard of work at height, so that protective equipment is not the main safeguard, and how will people nearby be kept clear? Evidence that answers it: the part of your method statement that shows those controls. **MUST PASS**
- **H3 (work at height)** How will work at height be authorised on our site, under our permit where we issue one, and how would you rescue someone if it went wrong? Evidence that answers it: a written plan for getting a person out safely during work at height, saying how and by whom, and the permit or written authorisation your people will work under.

People on this job: one row per certificate, with the columns person, role_on_job, certification, issuer, expires (YYYY-MM-DD).

Declaration: Signed on behalf of the contractor. Everything in this return, answers and documents, can be relied on as given.
Signed: ____________ Name: ____________ Position: ____________ Date: ____________
